# Style

Melodic house, 128 BPM, four-on-the-floor kick, rolling sub bass, minor key; no violins, no string section, pads only; English vocals. Intro: no drums, bass and pad playing block chords only, no melody, Bach chorale-style. Verse 1: male baritone, calm, serene, breathy, main melody, sparse groove. Verse 2: female alto, warm husky soulful, urgent but controlled, never shouting. Short interlude after verse 2: Intro ambience, Bach chorale-style. Then a looping instrumental starts, very nude and sparse at first, gradually introducing a trio of guzheng, pipa and erhu, building tension to the last verse. Over that building instrumental, a completely new celestial melody: female first half, male second half. Final verse, where the music reaches its climax: male and female in strict unison, original melody, no harmony, calm, serene, over drums, thick pads, the trio, heavy bassline. Abrupt end on held pad chord, quick fade-out.

# Exclude Styles

lounge, elevator music, muzak, easy listening, smooth jazz, cinematic, orchestral, string section, violins, choir, backing vocals, vocal harmonies, pop ballad, sentimental, lush chords, vibrato, shouting, rock guitars
